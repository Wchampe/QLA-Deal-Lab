# Source directory

Research baseline: 2026-09-07. Directory entries are not live feeds. All financial eligibility and current provider availability require confirmation.

| Source | Category | Access | Purpose |
|---|---|---|---|
| [BizBuySell](https://www.bizbuysell.com/content/search/) | Deals | Browser / authorized import | Main Street businesses; compare SDE definitions and syndicated duplicates. |
| [BizQuest](https://www.bizquest.com/buy-a-business-for-sale/page-1/) | Deals | Browser / authorized import | Businesses and broker listings; may overlap BizBuySell. |
| [BusinessesForSale](https://www.businessesforsale.com/) | Deals | Browser / authorized import | US and international businesses; keep currencies separate. |
| [Acquire.com](https://acquire.com/) | Digital | Account / authorized import | SaaS, ecommerce, agencies, apps and online businesses. |
| [Flippa](https://flippa.com/) | Digital | Account / authorized import | Online businesses and digital assets; distinguish assets from operating companies. |
| [Empire Flippers](https://support.empireflippers.com/hc/en-us/articles/202132306-How-do-I-buy-an-online-business-with-Empire-Flippers) | Digital | Account / authorized import | Digital acquisitions; identity/funds verification needed to unlock details. |
| [FE International](https://www.feinternational.com/) | Digital | Browser / adviser | Technology M&A adviser and capital-raising research. |
| [Sunbelt](https://www.sunbeltnetwork.com/) | Deals | Browser / authorized import | Business broker offices and listings. |
| [Transworld](https://www.tworld.com/) | Deals | Browser / authorized import | Broker network for acquisitions and exits. |
| [Murphy Business](https://murphybusiness.com/) | Deals | Browser / authorized import | Business brokerage, valuations, and lower middle market. |
| [Axial](https://www.axial.net/) | Buyers & capital | Membership / authorized import | Private North American deal network; no public listing search. Buyers, lenders, advisers, investors. |
| [SAM.gov API](https://open.gsa.gov/api/get-opportunities-public-api/) | Government | Live API / key required | Contract opportunities; not acquisition listings or guaranteed sales. |
| [USAspending API](https://api.usaspending.gov/docs/endpoints) | Government | Live API / no key | Federal awards and recipients; research concentration and potential targets. |
| [GSA surplus](https://www.gsa.gov/buy-through-us/government-property-for-sale-or-lease/office-of-personal-property-management/for-citizens-seeking-surplus-property) | Assets & distress | Browser / auction terms | Government surplus asset sales, not necessarily operating businesses. |
| [Public Surplus](https://www.publicsurplus.com/sms/browse/home) | Assets & distress | Browser / auction terms | Government surplus auctions; inspect condition, title, premiums and removal costs. |
| [PACER](https://pacer.uscourts.gov/) | Assets & distress | Account / authorized import | Federal court records; bankruptcy and sale-process research. Account and fees may apply. |
| [SBA 7(a)](https://www.sba.gov/loans/7a-loans/) | Funding | Program research | Ownership changes and other eligible uses. Published single-loan maximum $5M; verify all eligibility and terms. |
| [SBA Lender Match](https://www.sba.gov/loans/lender-match/) | Funding | Directory / application outside app | Introductions to participating lenders; not an application or guarantee of credit. |
| [SBA 504](https://www.sba.gov/loans/504-loans/) | Funding | Program research | Eligible fixed assets. Not working capital, inventory, or general goodwill acquisition funding. |
| [SBA combined financing update](https://legacy.sba.gov/article/2026/07/07/small-businesses-now-eligible-10-million-sba-financing) | Funding | Program research | July 2026 announcement on combining 7(a)/504. Not a single $10M 7(a) loan; confirm allocation with CDC/lender. |
| [SBA SBIC directory](https://www.sba.gov/funding-programs/investment-capital/sbic-directory) | Funding | Directory / CSV import | Privately managed licensed debt/equity investors. Filter active status, strategy, geography and check size. |
| [SBA investment capital](https://www.sba.gov/loans/additional-funding-opportunities/investment-capital/) | Funding | Program research | SBIC eligibility and funding overview; individual managers decide. |
| [USDA B&I](https://www.rd.usda.gov/programs-services/business-programs/business-industry-loan-guarantees-5) | Funding | Program research | Rural business lender guarantees; eligible acquisitions may maintain operations and create/save jobs. Texas page; use local office. |
| [USDA Business Programs](https://www.rd.usda.gov/programs-services/business-programs) | Funding | Directory | Rural programs and lender research. |
| [CDFI Fund](https://www.cdfifund.gov/programs-training/certification/cdfi) | Funding | Directory / download | Certified mission-driven institutions; mandate and acquisition lending vary. |
| [Treasury SSBCI](https://home.treasury.gov/policy-issues/small-business-programs/state-small-business-credit-initiative-ssbci) | Funding | Directory / program research | State/territorial/Tribal capital programs, loan participation and credit support; local rules. |
| [SEC capital pathways](https://www.sec.gov/resources-small-businesses/capital-raising-building-blocks/offering-pathways) | Funding | Reference | Equity capital-raising framework; counsel should select applicable offering route. |
| [IBBA broker directory](https://www.ibba.org/find-a-business-broker/) | Team | Directory | Search business brokers by geography, specialty and credentials. |
| [ACG](https://www.acg.org/membership-tools/why-join) | Buyers & capital | Membership / events | Middle market network of investors, lenders, operators and advisers. |
| [NACD](https://www.nacdonline.org/) | Team | Network | Board governance network and director education; investigate suitable advisers. |
| [Searchfunder](https://searchfunder.com/latest) | Buyers & capital | Account / network | Acquisition entrepreneurship community; research buyers, lenders and operators. |
| [SCORE](https://www.score.org/) | Team | Directory | Business mentoring and local resources; verify transaction expertise separately. |
| [SBA local assistance](https://www.sba.gov/counseling/local-assistance/) | Team | Directory | Local resource partners and business counseling. |
| [Dan Pena QLA resources](https://www.danpena.co.uk/downloads/) | Method | Reference | Primary QLA resources; use experienced team and operating discipline, not promises of no-money-down success. |
