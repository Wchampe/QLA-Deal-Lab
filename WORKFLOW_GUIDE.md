# Full deal workflow

The app's **Full Deal Workflow** tab creates a plan for the selected business. Choose a documented case as a reference lens; it does not mark your deal as complete. Each source reports only part of a real acquisition. The phase sequence, dates, gates and task lists are this project's recommended synthesis, not reported source timelines.

## Using the plan

1. Select a business, choose a case, set a planning start date, and create the 15-phase plan.
2. Assign named owners and realistic target dates. Initial day offsets are editable placeholders; search length and financing timing vary widely.
3. Complete tasks and record evidence references. Keep confidential documents in your own secure storage; this app holds references only.
4. Save work as In progress, Blocked, or Ready for review. Complete requires all dependencies, tasks, evidence or documented waivers, reviewer identity, and a review note.
5. Reopening a completed upstream phase flags completed dependent phases for re-review while preserving their evidence.
6. Export the Markdown playbook and JSON backup. The phase decision history is local and editable through backups; it is not a tamper-proof legal audit trail.

Work can occur in parallel: team-building and sourcing follow thesis; lender work can begin after structuring while LOI/diligence proceed. Closing requires both financing and definitive agreements. The pipeline stage field is a separate high-level label and does not automatically approve a workflow gate.

## Phase playbook

### 1. Acquisition thesis

Default role: Buyer / industry adviser. Prerequisites: None.

Completion gate: Approve a target profile and supportable capital budget.

- Choose industry, geography and size bands
- Define operating role, cash and outside-equity assumptions
- Record walk-away conditions and downside floor

Evidence items: Acquisition thesis; Buyer capital and experience summary.

### 2. Dream team and mandates

Default role: Buyer. Prerequisites: Acquisition thesis.

Completion gate: Assign accountable advisers and an operating lead.

- Identify chair/industry adviser and operator
- Engage transaction counsel and diligence accountant
- Record scopes, conflicts, references and decision authority

Evidence items: Team roles and engagement scopes; Conflicts and credentials review.

### 3. Sourcing and qualification

Default role: Marketplace Scout / buyer. Prerequisites: Acquisition thesis.

Completion gate: Retain a traceable target and plausible seller.

- Search marketplaces, brokers and industry referrals
- Deduplicate and record source/date and seller mandate
- Classify company sale versus asset or government signal

Evidence items: Source record and seller mandate; Initial target fit note.

### 4. Contact and information access

Default role: Buyer / counsel. Prerequisites: Sourcing and qualification.

Completion gate: Establish authorized access and screening evidence.

- Prepare and review initial inquiry
- Agree confidentiality and adviser access
- Request financials, ownership, assets and seller objectives

Evidence items: Confidentiality agreement or counsel-approved alternative; Initial information package.

### 5. Preliminary underwriting

Default role: Financial Underwriter / CPA. Prerequisites: Contact and information access.

Completion gate: Decide whether standalone economics justify further work.

- Normalize SDE/EBITDA and owner replacement costs
- Model uses, capex, taxes and working capital
- Stress earnings, rates and concentration; record missing data

Evidence items: Preliminary financial model; Earnings adjustments and evidence log.

### 6. Capital structure and valuation

Default role: Funding Matchmaker / buyer. Prerequisites: Preliminary underwriting, Dream team and mandates.

Completion gate: Select conditional financing and maximum supportable price.

- Compare debt, seller financing and equity
- Separate business, fixed-asset and working-capital uses
- Record guarantee, balloon, covenant and dilution questions

Evidence items: Sources and uses schedule; Financing route and valuation memo.

### 7. Indication of interest / LOI

Default role: Buyer / counsel. Prerequisites: Capital structure and valuation.

Completion gate: Agree the basis for diligence and conditional transaction terms.

- Define perimeter, price mechanism and working-capital basis
- Negotiate exclusivity, deposits, access and financing conditions
- Review binding and nonbinding provisions with counsel

Evidence items: Counsel-reviewed LOI or alternative offer structure; Diligence scope and timetable.

### 8. Confirmatory diligence

Default role: CPA / counsel / operator. Prerequisites: Indication of interest / LOI.

Completion gate: Resolve material financial, operating and legal issues.

- Reconcile bank, tax, accrual and related-party records
- Review customers, permits, staff, leases, cyber, claims and liens
- Quantify red flags, remediation, price changes and integration costs

Evidence items: Quality of earnings and working-capital analysis; Legal and operational diligence issue register.

### 9. Underwriting and commitments

Default role: Lender / capital specialist. Prerequisites: Capital structure and valuation.

Completion gate: Document funding and all remaining conditions.

- Provide application, buyer evidence and lender package
- Compare written debt/equity terms and verify eligibility
- Resolve equity evidence, seller-note treatment and conditions

Evidence items: Written debt and equity commitments; Lender conditions and equity evidence tracker.

### 10. Definitive agreements and consents

Default role: Transaction counsel. Prerequisites: Indication of interest / LOI, Confirmatory diligence.

Completion gate: Have reviewed agreements and a complete consent plan.

- Negotiate purchase agreement, disclosures and indemnities
- Secure landlord, franchisor, customer and regulatory consents as applicable
- Finalize transition, employment, seller-note and escrow terms

Evidence items: Definitive agreement and disclosure schedules; Third-party consent and transfer checklist.

### 11. Closing readiness and completion

Default role: Counsel / lender / buyer. Prerequisites: Underwriting and commitments, Definitive agreements and consents.

Completion gate: Close after accountable professionals confirm conditions.

- Reconcile final uses, adjustments, payoff and lien releases
- Independently verify payment instructions with known counterparties
- Record signed documents, receipts and transfer completion

Evidence items: Final closing statement and funds-flow approval; Executed closing set and transfer confirmations.

### 12. Day 1–30 stabilization

Default role: Acquisition operator. Prerequisites: Closing readiness and completion.

Completion gate: Keep cash, customers, people and operating access stable.

- Transfer banking controls, insurance, payroll and systems access
- Introduce ownership to staff and key customers
- Run weekly cash review and confirm seller handover

Evidence items: Day-one readiness and access checklist; 30-day cash and customer retention review.

### 13. Day 31–100 integration

Default role: Operator / integration lead. Prerequisites: Day 1–30 stabilization.

Completion gate: Measure results against underwriting and settle adjustments.

- Compare actual margins, cash, churn and capex with forecasts
- Complete working-capital true-up and integration milestones
- Close operational gaps and assign improvement experiments

Evidence items: 100-day operating scorecard; Post-close adjustment and integration register.

### 14. Ownership, improvement and add-ons

Default role: Operator / board. Prerequisites: Day 31–100 integration.

Completion gate: Demonstrate repeatable operations before expanding leverage.

- Review covenants, cash runway and leadership capacity
- Validate improvements with actual results
- Screen add-ons for standalone economics and integration capacity

Evidence items: Quarterly board and lender reporting; Add-on thesis or organic growth plan.

### 15. Exit preparation and sale

Default role: Owner / M&A adviser / counsel. Prerequisites: Ownership, improvement and add-ons.

Completion gate: Prepare a supportable exit and reconcile post-sale obligations.

- Build buyer universe, normalized earnings and transferable management
- Run controlled buyer diligence and compare offers
- Model debt payoff, costs, tax advice and post-sale obligations

Evidence items: Exit readiness and buyer comparison; Exit agreement and final proceeds reconciliation.

## Practical review templates

Use the templates folder for an information request list, financing comparison, diligence issue register, closing conditions and first-100-days scorecard. They are planning worksheets, not legal agreements. Counsel should prepare or review the actual NDA, LOI, purchase agreement, notes, guarantees, security documents and consent instruments.
