The two QLA-inspired choices in Off-market intelligence contain original app wording. They are not quotations, official Dan Peña templates, or an endorsement. Personalization comes from the dossier and the buyer's own input.

The scans above are hosted by a third party. Dan Peña's [official downloads](https://www.danpena.co.uk/downloads/) and [QLA library](https://www.danpena.co.uk/qla/) provide author-published resources; some book downloads require sign-up.

The official downloads page describes its Deal Flow resource as templates/frameworks/guides, but the [linked ZIP](https://www.danpena.co.uk/wp-content/uploads/deal-flow.zip), inspected during this update, contains two MP3 recordings, Side A and Side B. It did not contain written letter templates. The recordings were not used as the source for the app's email text.

The adaptations use the buyer's entered background. They do not insert claims about an established board, committed funding, prior conversations, signed confidentiality agreements, or attachments. An announced or completed transaction continues to receive the existing availability-focused question regardless of template selection.
