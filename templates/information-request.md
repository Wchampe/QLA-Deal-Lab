# Initial acquisition information request

Business: [name] | Buyer: [name] | Date: [date] | NDA / permission: [reference]

Request only what is appropriate for the phase and confidentiality arrangements.

- Three fiscal years of financial statements and tax returns; current year-to-date and prior-year comparable period.
- Monthly profit/loss and balance sheets; bank reconciliation and debt schedule.
- Earnings adjustments with evidence, owner duties and compensation, related-party transactions and standalone costs.
- Customer revenue/concentration, recurring contracts, backlog, churn, receivable aging and collection history.
- Inventory detail, obsolete stock, payable aging, seasonal working capital, and included/excluded assets.
- Equipment condition, maintenance capex, leases, key supplier and customer agreements.
- Ownership, authority to sell, liens, litigation, licenses, permits, insurance and required transfer consents.
- Employee roles and aggregate compensation, retention issues, benefits and key-person dependence.
- Systems, intellectual property ownership, software licenses, cybersecurity incidents, and access-transfer plan.
- Seller objectives, proposed perimeter, transition availability, and possible seller financing terms.

For government contractors: contract award vs funded backlog, revenue actually recognized, contract terms, change-of-control/novation analysis, set-aside status and customer concentration. Award totals are not acquisition cash flow.

For a franchise: franchisor approval, transfer/renewal fees, remaining term, lease consent and lender conditions.

For a regulated business: appropriate specialist reviews and required operating approvals before transfer.
